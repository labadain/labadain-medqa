Example 1:
English: What is the relationship between very low Mg2+ levels, PTH levels, and Ca2+ levels?

Tetun: nivel Mg2+ ne'ebé tun tebes korresponde ho nivel PTH ne'ebé tun, ne'ebé ikus mai rezulta nivel Ca2+ ne'ebé tun.
